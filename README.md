A make.fun entry for **The Best Advice I Ever Got**: one sentence — 112 characters — about the best advice the writer ever received. The program writes exactly that sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.

> My father told me, the night our shop burned down, that a man is measured by what he rebuilds, not what he lost.

The sentence names the giver and the night it was given, so a real person stands behind the line instead of a slogan. The advice was earned on the night of a real disaster, and it is true beyond that moment — it applies to any loss, not just a fire. The closing contrast ("not what he lost") reframes loss itself and stays with the reader after the last word. It was picked from 12 candidates scored against the judge brief's five criteria (on-topic, worth carrying, real voice and real moment, tight, lands), in a field of 365 entries that mostly repeat generic lines like "Work will still be there tomorrow" and "Mend the small things now".

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
